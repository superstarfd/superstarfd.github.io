About us

The future of finance support providing expert, embedded and effortlessly scalable finance department solutions

Siân started Superstar FD in 2024 after qualifying with ACCA in 2007 and spending many years running in-house finance departments. What began as providing outsourced CFO and FD support quickly evolved, after she realised that clients needed more than just strategic advice, they needed the capability of a full finance department. So she built a team and created a service that acts as a seamless extension of your business. Superstar FD doesn't just provide outsourced support, it is your finance department.

When she’s not helping clients make sense of their numbers, you’ll find her outdoors, muddy boots and all. She dabbles in gardening (unsuccessfully), adores animals (even being a gerbil rescue volunteer) and believes that staying grounded and curious makes her better at working with her clients.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to give services firms a fully staffed finance department, delivering expert support, better data, improved cashflow and strategic insight - all at a fraction of the cost.
